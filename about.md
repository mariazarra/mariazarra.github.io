<section class="page-heading about-heading">
  <h1>Maria Zarra</h1>
  <p>
    Maria Zarra is a psychology researcher and educator based in Amsterdam, with academic
    training in social and organizational psychology, education, and research
    methods. Her work is grounded in the study of cooperation, decision-making,
    emotion, communication, and the social conditions that shape human behavior.
  </p>
</section>

<section class="about-profile" aria-labelledby="academic-profile">
  <div class="about-intro">
    <h2 id="academic-profile">Academic Profile</h2>
    <p>
      Maria's background combines a Research Master's in Social Psychology and a
      Bachelor's in Psychology at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam with an earlier
      Bachelor's degree in Education from the University of Thessaly. This
      academic path shapes Mind in Review's focus on evidence-based psychology:
      how anxiety, stress, cognition, memory, sleep, behavior, mental health,
      neuroscience, consciousness, and relationships can be understood through
      both scientific research and everyday human experience.
    </p>
    <p>
      Her writing brings academic concepts into clear language, connecting
      social and organizational psychology with questions people often meet in
      daily life: why emotions become overwhelming, how attention and memory
      guide behavior, what work and social environments do to mental health, and
      how psychological knowledge can support more thoughtful self-understanding.
    </p>
