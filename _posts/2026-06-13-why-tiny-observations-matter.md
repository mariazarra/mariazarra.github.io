Psychology often begins before theory. It begins with noticing a small moment
clearly enough that it becomes meaningful.

A person might notice that they apologize before asking for what they need.
Someone else might notice that they become quiet when a conversation turns
uncertain. Another person might find that a certain kind of criticism follows
them for hours.

These details can seem small, but they are useful because they reveal patterns.
Once a pattern is visible, it can be named, questioned, and sometimes gently
changed.

This blog will use that kind of attention as a starting point: careful, curious,
and practical.
